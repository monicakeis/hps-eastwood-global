Eastwood Global // The Partnership

Real school.
Real wellbeing.

Eastwood Global has partnered with High Performance Systems, a high performance education provider that has worked with student athletes and sport academies including Desert Vipers, Empire Rugby Academy and Aspire Gymnastics. HPS is now Eastwood Global's official high performance education partner, delivering a live, weekly curriculum in performance nutrition and performance mindset.

This is not a wellness add-on bolted onto an existing timetable. It is the same model Eastwood Global already built for its elite student athletes: live small classes, flexible scheduling, and a structure that works around a student's training calendar rather than against it. This partnership extends that promise from timetable to curriculum, teaching the habits behind performance with the same rigour as any other subject.

Eastwood Global // Questions

Before you book

01Is this only for elite athletes?
No. The weekly session is part of the school day for Eastwood Global students aged 11 to 18. Competitive athletes feel the need first, because their margins for a poorly managed week are thinner, but the habits taught apply to any student working under pressure.
02Does this replace PE, or my child's own club training?
No. HPS coaches teach the habits behind performance, mindset and fuelling, rather than delivering physical training. Your child's own coaches, club and training programme stay exactly as they are. The school works around them.
03Is it a separate platform or log-in?
No. Sessions run live inside the existing Eastwood Global timetable, in the same place as every other class.
04What if my child does not train competitively?
Nothing changes. The session is included for every enrolled student in the age range, and the material is written for young people in general, not for a specific sport.
05How does it work across time zones?
Eastwood Global runs two global timetables built to serve students in different regions, with a 1:12 class ratio in live classes. Admissions will map your child's training and competition calendar against both during the discovery call.
